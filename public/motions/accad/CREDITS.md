# Captured martial-arts kicks

Source: Open Motion Project, ACCAD / The Ohio State University.
https://accad.osu.edu/research/motion-lab/mocap-system-and-data

License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Male2_G2_FrontKick.bvh and Male2_G4_SpinningBackKick.bvh were downloaded
from the official Male2 BVH archive. Adaptations by this project: trimmed
performances, anatomical-frame retargeting to Leo's proportions, closed-finger
grips, removal of travel drift, stage-height correction and gameplay timing.

The original captures are not claimed as our work or as AAA game animations.
leonardo-kicks.glb includes the existing Quaternius CC0 motions and project's
character mesh in addition to the adapted ACCAD kicks. See the model credits.

Rebuild: node tools/retarget-kicks.mjs; node tools/export-kick-review-glb.mjs
Editable Blender asset: art/leonardo-mocap-kicks.blend.
